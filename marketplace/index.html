Process

Go Solar in 5 Simple Steps

From idea to installation — our platform handles the complexity so you can focus on your energy goals.

01

Create Your RFQ

Tell us your energy needs — location, system size, budget, and timeline. Our AI-powered form makes it easy.

02

Get Matched Instantly

Our matching engine scores and ranks the top 3–5 verified installers near you within 30 seconds.

03

Compare & Choose

Review side-by-side bid comparisons — price, timeline, SunlitScore, certifications, and reviews.

04

Sign & Fund Escrow

Sign the auto-generated digital contract and fund the escrow via Paystack or Flutterwave. Funds are safe until you approve.

05

Track & Approve

Monitor installation in real-time. Approve each milestone to release payment. Rate your installer when done.
